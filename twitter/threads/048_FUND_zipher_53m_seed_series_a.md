## 1
Zipher raised $53M across its seed and Series A, from TLV Partners, Maor Investments and Glilot Capital. Its AI agents decide how a company's data jobs run: which engine, which resources, when. Customers give it code, an SLA and a budget.

## 2
Results from Zipher's case studies: OpenWeb cut Databricks spending 59%, Yotpo cut annual costs 39% and saw savings in 2 weeks. Hertz says compute costs fell 25% to 40%, depending on the workload. Zipher claims 40 to 60% across Fortune 500 customers, without defining the term.

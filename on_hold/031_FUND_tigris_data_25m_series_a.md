# Tigris Data Raises $25M Series A to Build Object Storage for AI

## Technical Overview

- Startup: Tigris Data ([tigrisdata.com](https://www.tigrisdata.com/))
- Amount Raised: $25M
- Funding Round: Series A, [announced October 8, 2025](https://www.tigrisdata.com/blog/series-a/)
- Main Investors (known): Spark Capital (lead), Andreessen Horowitz
- Problem Area: Globally distributed, S3-compatible object storage for AI workloads

---

## The Problem

Big clouds charge to move data out. For AI teams that run GPUs across several providers, that egress fee can become a large part of the bill. A customer quoted by [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/09/this-distributed-data-storage-startup-wants-to-take-on-big-cloud/), the head of engineering at fal.ai, says egress costs once accounted for the majority of its cloud spending. That is one customer's claim.

---

## How They Solve It

Tigris is an S3-compatible, globally distributed object storage service. In its own words, it lets developers "store and process training data at scale, serve models with low latency anywhere in the world, and move freely across clouds without paying the cloud tax of egress fees". Because it speaks the S3 API, existing S3 libraries keep working.

The notable part is the infrastructure. In 2024 the company said it was [building on top of Fly.io](https://www.tigrisdata.com/blog/a16z-round-press-release/). Now it says it is "operating on its own infrastructure" and will spend the new money on deploying its own hardware in data centers worldwide. TechCrunch reports three data centers today, in Virginia, Chicago and San Jose, with London, Frankfurt and Singapore planned.

The founders, Ovais Tariq, Himank Chaudhary and Yevgeniy Firsov, come from Uber's storage team. Named customers in the Series A post include fal, Krea, Hedra, Fly.io and Beam. TechCrunch says Tigris has more than 4,000 customers, and quotes the CEO saying it has grown 8x every year. Both are company figures.

## Previous Rounds and Capital Trajectory

- Seed - Mar 2024 - amount not disclosed in the announcement, led by Andreessen Horowitz - [Tigris](https://www.tigrisdata.com/blog/a16z-round-press-release/)
- Series A - Oct 2025 - $25M, led by Spark Capital, with Andreessen Horowitz - [Tigris](https://www.tigrisdata.com/blog/series-a/), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/09/this-distributed-data-storage-startup-wants-to-take-on-big-cloud/)

Data aggregators list an earlier seed of about $6.35M dated December 2021, but I could not confirm it from a primary source, and it conflicts with the company's own March 2024 announcement. Total raised is not stated by the company.

---

## Why This Round Matters

It is a bet that AI workloads want storage that follows the GPUs across clouds, and that a startup can run its own hardware to do it. That is capital-heavy for a storage company.

## 1
ClickHouse acquired Xata, the Postgres platform known for instant database branching. No price disclosed.

I wrote my thoughts: https://itayb-dt.github.io/data_landscape/blog_posts/acq/037_ACQ_xata_by_clickhouse.html

## 2
TLDR: Snowflake (Crunchy), Databricks (Neon) and ClickHouse (Xata) now each own a strong Postgres offering, built to work with their analytics engine. How well the operational and analytics stores sync is the thing to watch.

## 3
It's also a talent story. Building a Postgres OLTP layer is hard, and few teams have done it. Each of the three bought a team that already has, and Xata's founders now join ClickHouse. Of the three, I think ClickHouse leads on open source.

Disclosure: I'm a small investor in Honeydew and a friend of one of the founders.

Workday announced it will acquire Honeydew, a semantic layer that gives AI agents and BI tools the same business definitions. Price not disclosed.

## 2
Ask "what is our headcount?" and the answer depends on a definition. Workday's CTO: "An AI model shouldn't be improvising the calculation behind a payroll number." Honeydew compiles the same question into the same query every time.

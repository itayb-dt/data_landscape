# Prompt: Thread for a funding round article

Read [_tweet_rules.md](_tweet_rules.md) first.

Input: the funding article (`blog_posts/fund/...`).

Write a thread of 2 or 3 posts.

1. **The news:** company, amount, round, lead investor, and in one phrase what the company builds. End with the link. Only state a valuation if the article does.
2. **The point:** the main idea of the article's Summary (seed and Series A) or "Why This Round Matters" (Series B and later): the problem and why the money is going there.
3. **Seed and Series A only:** the founders and their background, if the article has it, in one or two sentences. For Series B and later, use post 3 for what was announced with the round (an acquisition, a product launch), or stop at post 2.

No My Take verdict unless the article has one.

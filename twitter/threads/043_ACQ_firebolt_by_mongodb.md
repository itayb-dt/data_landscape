## 1
MongoDB acquired Firebolt, a cloud analytics database built for fast queries from many users at once. The price was not disclosed. CTech estimates it is significantly below Firebolt's $1.4B valuation from 2022.

https://itayb-dt.github.io/data_landscape/blog_posts/acq/043_ACQ_firebolt_by_mongodb.html

## 2
Analytics today means querying a petabyte or trillions of rows in seconds or less. If a database can't do that, customers copy the data to another one: more cost, more complexity, more things that can break. Firebolt's engine will run next to MongoDB and stay in sync.

## 3
I think Firebolt was a zombie unicorn for a long time. Both founders left in 2025 and there were two rounds of layoffs. To me this looks like a sad exit, an acquihire with a bit of technology on top.

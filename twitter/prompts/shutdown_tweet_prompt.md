# Prompt: Thread for a shutdown article

Read [_tweet_rules.md](_tweet_rules.md) first.

Input: the shutdown article (`blog_posts/shutdown/...`).

Write a thread of 2 or 3 posts. Be respectful: a company closed, people lost jobs. No mockery, no "I told you so".

1. **The news:** the company, that it is shutting down or insolvent, what it tried to do, and how much it raised if the article says. End with the link.
2. **Why it failed:** the reason, as the article gives it, in plain words.
3. **The author's view** from My Take, in "I think" form.

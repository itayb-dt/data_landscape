## 1
definity, a Chicago startup, raised a $12M Series A led by GreatPoint Ventures. It puts AI agents inside Spark pipelines to cut cost and stop bad runs while the job is still running.

https://itayb-dt.github.io/data_landscape/blog_posts/fund/041_FUND_definity_12m_series_a.html

## 2
Most pipeline tools report after a job finishes. definity runs an agent inside the Spark driver, so it can change resources or stop a job midway. Nexxen, a customer, says it cut troubleshooting and optimization effort by 70%.

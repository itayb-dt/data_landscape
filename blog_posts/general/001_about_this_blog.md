Data/Landscape is a site about the data and AI market: who is buying whom, who is raising money, who is shutting down, and what it means. I read the announcements, check the facts, and add my own view.

## Why This Site Matters

Most discussions about data focus on the tactical: which tools to use, how to optimize pipelines, or the latest machine learning frameworks. While these topics have their place, they miss the bigger picture. The real challenge isn't technical, it's strategic. How do you build data systems that survive organizational changes, market shifts, and the relentless pace of technological evolution?

This site aims to bridge that gap with a high-level, strategic view of the data landscape from someone who has been both a builder and a product leader inside a large company.

## What You'll Find Here

- **Acquisitions, funding rounds, shutdowns and mergers:** who is buying whom, why, and what it signals about market direction. Every article follows the same structure, so you can find the problem, the solution and my take quickly.
- **Concepts and database reviews:** short explanations of ideas such as separating storage and compute, and of databases worth knowing.
- **Market Moves:** the front page, a timeline of everything I track, including events I haven't written about yet.

Facts come with links to the original announcements. Vendor claims are labeled as claims, and my own analysis is labeled as mine.

## On Timing and Perspective

I usually write about an announcement a few days after it happens, enough time to read the primary sources and separate signal from noise. When something develops later, I add a dated entry under Updates at the bottom of the article instead of rewriting it. In a world of instant hot takes, I'd rather be accurate than first.

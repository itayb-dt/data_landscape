# Prompt: Thread for an acquisition article

Read [_tweet_rules.md](_tweet_rules.md) first.

Input: the acquisition article (`blog_posts/acq/...`).

Write a thread of 2 or 3 posts.

1. **The news:** who acquired whom, and in one phrase what the target does. Add the price only if the article states it. End with the link.
2. **TLDR:** the one point of the article, taken from "Understanding the Acquisition": why the buyer wanted it, or what it changes. One idea.
3. **The author's view** (only if the article has a My Take): the central opinion, in "I think" form. If there is no My Take, stop at post 2.

Example (Xata): see [threads/037_ACQ_xata_by_clickhouse.md](../threads/037_ACQ_xata_by_clickhouse.md).

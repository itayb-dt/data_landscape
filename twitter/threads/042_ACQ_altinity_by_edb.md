## 1
EDB, a Postgres company, is acquiring Altinity, a leading independent provider of software, services and support for ClickHouse. Price not disclosed.

https://itayb-dt.github.io/data_landscape/blog_posts/acq/042_ACQ_altinity_by_edb.html

## 2
EDB gets an analytics engine to sit next to its Postgres, plus the people who know how to run it. Its own line: "Postgres writes the record. ClickHouse reads it."

## 3
I think ClickHouse's open source base let companies like Altinity grow, but not by much. EDB turned open source Postgres into an enterprise product. Now it has open source ClickHouse. Time will tell.

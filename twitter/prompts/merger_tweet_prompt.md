# Prompt: Thread for a merger article

Read [_tweet_rules.md](_tweet_rules.md) first.

Input: the merger article (`blog_posts/merger/...`).

Write a thread of 2 or 3 posts.

1. **The news:** the two companies, that they merge as equals (not an acquisition), and the form of the deal if the article gives it. End with the link.
2. **What each does and why they merged:** one sentence per company, then the reason in the companies' own words.
3. **The author's view** from My Take, in "I think" form.

# Prompt: Thread for a concept, database review, tech review or random-thoughts article

Read [_tweet_rules.md](_tweet_rules.md) first.

Input: the article (`blog_posts/cncpt/`, `db/`, `techrev/` or `random/`).

Write a thread of 1 to 3 posts.

1. **The hook:** the question the article answers or the claim it makes, in plain words, then the link. For a concept, say what it is in one sentence. For a database or tech review, say what it is and who it is for. For random thoughts, state the opinion.
2. **The main point:** the one thing a reader should remember, from the article's decision framework, "best for" or central argument.
3. **A concrete detail:** one number, example or tradeoff from the article. Skip it if post 2 already covers it.

Do not summarize the whole article. One idea per post.

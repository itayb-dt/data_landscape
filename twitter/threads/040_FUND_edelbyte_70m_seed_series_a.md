## 1
Israeli startup Edelbyte raised $70M in seed and Series A funding, led by Sequoia and Quiet Capital. It builds search and analytics for AI agents.

Details: https://itayb-dt.github.io/data_landscape/blog_posts/fund/040_FUND_edelbyte_70m_seed_series_a.html

## 2
The problem is cost at petabyte scale. An agent can fire fifty queries where a person runs one, so companies keep less history or ask fewer questions. Edelbyte claims ten times the queries for the same budget.

## 3
The founders built Kusto at Microsoft, the engine behind Sentinel, Defender and Azure Monitor, sold to customers as Azure Data Explorer. The company hasn't said how its own engine works, but I'd guess it's similar.

## 4
Kusto was named after Jacques Cousteau, because it was built for diving into oceans of data.
